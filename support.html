Frequently Asked

How do I restore my purchases?
Open the game, go to Settings → Shop → Restore Purchases.

I bought Remove Ads / Double Coins but don't see it.
Tap Restore Purchases in Settings. If it still doesn't appear, email us with the date of purchase and we'll help.

Will I lose my progress?
Your progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app erases it. Purchases can always be restored via Restore Purchases.

How do ads work?
Ads are optional. Watching one grants an in-game bonus (a boost, time-skip, etc.). You can remove ads entirely with the Remove Ads purchase.
