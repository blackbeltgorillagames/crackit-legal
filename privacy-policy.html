Privacy Policy for Crack It

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Crack It (“the App”, “we”, “us”), published by Setitupsolutions, handles information when you use it. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

Summary

Crack It is a single-player idle game. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, or contacts. The App does, however, show advertisements and offer in-app purchases, and those features rely on third parties (Google and Apple) that may collect limited technical data as described below.

Information Collected

Game progress. Your progress (coins, pets, upgrades, and settings) is stored only on your device. We do not upload or have access to it.

Advertising data. The App uses Google AdMob to display rewarded advertisements. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process technical information such as a device advertising identifier (IDFA), device type and operating system, coarse location inferred from IP address, and ad interaction events. On first launch the App asks, via Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether you allow tracking. If you decline, ads are shown in a non-personalized (contextual) form. Watching ads is always optional and is never required to play.

Purchase data. In-app purchases (Remove Ads, Double Coins, Supporter Bundle) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment information; we never see or store your payment details. We only receive confirmation of whether a purchase was completed.

Diagnostics. Apple and Google may collect aggregated, anonymized crash and performance data to help keep the App and their SDKs working. We do not combine this with any identity.

How Information Is Used

  • To display and measure advertisements (via Google AdMob).
  • To deliver in-app purchases and restore them (via Apple).
  • To diagnose crashes and improve stability.

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third parties, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit — https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Your Choices

  • Tracking: You can change tracking permission any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Personalized ads: You can limit ad personalization in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  • Remove ads: Purchasing “Remove Ads” (or the Supporter Bundle) stops ads from being shown.
  • Reset data: Deleting the App removes all locally stored game progress from your device.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will address it.

Data Retention

Game data lives only on your device for as long as the App is installed. Any advertising or diagnostic data collected by Google or Apple is retained according to their respective policies.
